CallSense — Speech Emotion Review

An engineering prototype for reviewing speech emotion estimates from recorded audio using local emotion2vec+ inference. Audio stays exclusively in memory on loopback.

Open Analysis Workspace →
Responsible Use Notice: Audio is processed locally in memory and never persisted or sent to external servers. Model outputs are uncalibrated vocal navigation aids, not legal evidence or deception detection.

How It Works

Local, transparent acoustic review pipeline designed for recorded audio review.

01

Local Audio Ingestion

Accepts WAV or MP3 files up to 20 MiB and 10 minutes. Audio stays strictly in memory on loopback with zero external telemetry or cloud storage.

02

5-Second Window Inference

Segments audio into chronological five-second windows, analyzing acoustic features using local emotion2vec+ models without simulated predictions.

03

Timeline Navigation & Radar

Seek directly to segments in the native audio player, inspect raw uncalibrated scores, and review aggregate emotion distributions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding model boundaries and prototype capabilities.

Is this a lie detector or guilt detector?

No. CallSense never predicts truth, deception, intent, urgency, credibility, or guilt. Vocal emotion scores reflect acoustic patterns and cannot determine whether a statement is true or false.

Does audio get stored or uploaded?

No. The server binds strictly to 127.0.0.1 (loopback). Audio is read in bounded memory, analyzed, and purged immediately. There is no database or disk persistence.

Are the emotion scores probabilities?

No. Output scores are raw uncalibrated model outputs from emotion2vec+. They provide relative navigation cues to assist human review, not calibrated probabilities.
