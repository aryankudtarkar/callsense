Truth and lie assessment

The current voice model was built to estimate speech emotion labels. It does not assess whether a statement is true or false.

Truth / lie percentage Not available

CallSense cannot produce a defensible truthfulness percentage from emotion scores. Stress, calmness, or another vocal label is not evidence that someone is lying or telling the truth.
